Overview

Project overview

This project focused on the design and experimental evaluation of a PLC-based hydraulic fixture for vibration suppression during thin-wall machining. Our team designed and built the fixture and hydraulic platform, and used a PLC-based PID controller to regulate the hydraulic support force.

Feedforward compensation was applied to address the system’s response delay. The control algorithm reduced the steady-state pressure error from ±2.5 bar to within ±1 bar and shortened the step response time from over 3 seconds to under 0.5 seconds.

Impact hammer tests and cutting experiments were conducted to investigate the workpiece–fixture system’s dynamic characteristics and evaluate vibration suppression under different support conditions.
