Project overview

This ongoing research explores three-dimensional motion control of magnetic microrobots for dental applications. It brings together a handheld coil device, robotic-arm positioning, magnetic-field simulation, and real-time visual tracking.

My contributions

  • Designed a handheld device for coil-based microrobot control.
  • Simulated magnetic flux density, magnetic forces, and motion trajectories.
  • Investigated current-control strategies for three-dimensional motion.
  • Integrated a robotic arm to position the control device.
  • Implemented AprilTag-based tracking of real-time device position.

Technical approach

Magnetic actuation provides the basis for microrobot motion, while robotic positioning controls the device location. AprilTag tracking supplies visual position information for the feedback system.
